About Chidiya Ud

Chidiya Ud is a traditional Indian listening and reaction game, usually played in a group. One person leads the round, naming birds, animals, objects, and anything else they can think of.

Traditional group rules

Players react when they hear something that can fly and stay still for everything else. The fun is in listening closely instead of following along too quickly.

How this version works

Place your finger on the table to begin. Keep holding for things that cannot fly, and lift your finger as soon as a flying thing is called.

Controls

Touch or mouse: Place and keep your finger on the table. Lift it when the item can fly.

Keyboard: Keep Space or Enter pressed. Release the key when the item can fly.

Play by sight or sound: Each item appears on screen and is also spoken aloud.
